## Headline corrections

1. **Tenants cannot apply for Targeted Financial Assistance (TFA) directly.** The landlord initiates Philadelphia's Eviction Diversion Program and applies for TFA; the tenant completes an affidavit and must be below 80% of Area Median Income. Any entry telling a tenant to "apply for TFA" should be corrected to the landlord-initiated route. PHDC figures: arrears up to $3,500 plus up to two months' forward rent; $75 million provided March 2023–December 2025.
2. **Clarifi does not pay rent.** Its own site states it "cannot assist directly with … providing monetary rental assistance." It provides rental counselling and help with emergency-assistance applications (1635 Market St., Suite 510, Philadelphia, PA 19103; (215) 563-5665).
3. **Affordable Housing Centers of Pennsylvania counsels; it does not pay rent.** It provides HUD-approved housing counselling and Eviction Diversion mediation support (846 N. Broad St., Philadelphia, PA 19130; (215) 765-1221).
4. **Philadelphia Housing Authority waitlists are closed.** The Housing Choice Voucher waitlist last opened January 23–February 5, 2023; the public-housing waitlist closed April 15, 2013. Secondary trackers conflict on a reopening date (December 31, 2026 vs. "not until 2027"); no reopening date is confirmed here.
5. **COVID-era rental assistance (PHLRentAssist / ERA) is closed, not current help.** PHDC closed the portal in January 2022 and a city report states the programme ended in 2023. Current successors are Eviction Diversion/TFA and FreshStartPHL.
6. **FreshStartPHL exists but its open status on 2026-11-01 is unverified.** PHDC's July 2026 RFP confirms up to $4,800 to landlords plus up to $1,000 to tenants for moves after eviction/displacement (launched January 2025). Do not present it as currently open without a fresh PHDC programme-page check.
7. **TURN / the Philly Tenant Hotline is a rights and counselling service, not a general rent fund** (100 S. Broad St., Suite 800, Philadelphia, PA 19110; (215) 940-3900; hotline (267) 443-2500). Any cash help is conditional and referral-based.
8. **Neighbourhood and population limits matter.** Germantown Avenue Crisis Ministry serves only zip codes 19118, 19119, 19126, 19138, 19144, 19150. WRAP serves only recent hires in hard-to-fill City / School District / Charter School roles. PhillySEEDS' deposit match (up to $2,000) serves only PHA voucher participants approved to move. VMC SSVF serves only very low-income veterans.
9. **Do not double-count:** Eviction Diversion = TFA pathway (one programme); PHA "Accessible Housing Information" = PHA itself; UESF general help + UESF SSVF = one organisation, two programmes; VMC SSVF + Project HOME's historic PASSVF collaborative = one federal SSVF stream, routed via VMC.
10. **Out-of-county offices excluded:** Clarifi Upper Darby (Delaware County) and Jenkintown (Montgomery County), and VMC's Downingtown suburban office (Chester County), all surface in Philadelphia-area searches but are not Philadelphia County services.

## Method and limits

- PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven; text fetch returned statewide results, not a Philadelphia-filtered list. Philadelphia items were extracted from those pages and cross-checked against organisations' own sites where possible. This pilot is therefore NOT a complete directory — it is a checked pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus clearly-labelled secondary lists.
- No funding, waitlist-open, or application-open status is presented as current unless the organisation's own site said so on 2026-11-01.
- No organisation is called closed or defunct on the basis of a failed page load; incomplete checks are marked UNVERIFIED.
- Address/phone conflicts (UESF street address; UCSEP, ACHIEVEability and Intercultural Family Services details) are flagged in the full report's pre-send re-check list and must be resolved before any send.
- No residency claim is made about the auditor.
