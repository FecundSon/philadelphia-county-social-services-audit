# Philadelphia County, PA — Housing / rental assistance audit

- County: Philadelphia County, PA (city and county coterminous)
- Category: Housing / rental assistance
- Date checked: 2026-11-01
- Method: public web only — browser search and page-text fetch. No live browser tasks, no sign-ins, no purchases, no contact with any organisation. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator.
- Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
- Status: **NOT SENT** — staged only. Nothing in this report has been submitted to PA 211 or to any organisation.
- Auditor note: the auditor is not presented as a resident of Philadelphia County. No residency claim is made anywhere in this report.

## Headline findings

1. **Philadelphia's rent help is mostly city programmes plus neighbourhood-restricted charities — not a single general rent fund.** The citywide pillars are OHS Prevention, Diversion and Intake (overdue rent / security deposit, funding-dependent), PHDC Eviction Diversion / Targeted Financial Assistance (landlord-initiated, arrears + forward rent), PHDC FreshStartPHL (move-in costs after eviction/displacement), and the PHA voucher/public-housing system — whose waitlists are closed.
2. **The most consequential correction is about who can apply.** Tenants cannot apply for Targeted Financial Assistance directly — the landlord initiates Eviction Diversion and applies for TFA. Any directory entry that tells a tenant to "apply for TFA" is misleading and should be corrected to the landlord-initiated route.
3. **Two well-known names do NOT pay rent.** Clarifi's own site states it "cannot assist directly with … providing monetary rental assistance" — it counsels and helps with applications. AHCOPA likewise counsels and mediates; it is not a rent fund. TURN (Philly Tenant Hotline) is a rights/counselling service; any cash help is conditional and referral-based, not a walk-in fund.
4. **PHA waitlists are closed, and a supposed December 2026 reopening is unconfirmed.** Secondary trackers checked in October 2026 agree the Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is closed (last opened January 23–February 5, 2023) and the public-housing waitlist closed April 15, 2013, but they conflict on reopening (December 31, 2026 vs. "not until 2027"). No reopening date is confirmed on PHA's own site in this check and none is published here as fact.
5. **COVID-era rental assistance is over and must not be listed as current help.** PHLRentAssist / ERA disbursed about $300 million to about 46,000 families; PHDC closed the portal in January 2022 and a city report states the programme ended in 2023. Its successors are Eviction Diversion/TFA and FreshStartPHL.
6. **FreshStartPHL exists but its open/closed status on 2026-11-01 is unverified.** PHDC's July 2026 RFP confirms the programme (up to $4,800 to landlords, up to $1,000 to tenants, launched January 2025), but no same-day PHDC programme-page confirmation that applications are open was retrieved. Do not present it as currently open.
7. **Four listings are secondary-only and unverified on their own sites:** Salvation Army Greater Philadelphia, Intercultural Family Services, United Communities Southeast Philadelphia, and ACHIEVEability. Each also carries an address or phone conflict (see re-check list). None is called closed or defunct — all are UNVERIFIED.
8. **Neighbourhood and population restrictions are the norm, not the exception.** GACM serves only six Northwest zip codes; ACHIEVEability focuses on Haddington/Cobbs Creek; WRAP serves only recent hires in hard-to-fill City/School District/Charter roles; PhillySEEDS serves only PHA voucher participants approved to move; VMC SSVF serves only very low-income veterans; Valley Youth House's Pride rental help serves LGBTQ youth 18–21 by referral; Action Wellness's subsidy serves people living with HIV/chronic illness; Human Good buildings serve seniors 62+.
9. **Duplicates that must not be double-counted:** Eviction Diversion = TFA pathway (one programme); PHA "Accessible Housing Information" = PHA itself; UESF general help + UESF SSVF = one organisation, two programmes; VMC SSVF + Project HOME's historic PASSVF collaborative = one federal SSVF stream, routed via VMC.
10. **Out-of-county traps are real.** Clarifi's Upper Darby (Delaware County) and Jenkintown (Montgomery County) offices, and VMC's Downingtown (Chester County) suburban office, all surface in Philadelphia-area searches. A Philadelphia postal address alone does not settle county — exact addresses were used, and those three offices are excluded as OUT OF SCOPE.

## What this report does NOT claim

- This is NOT a complete directory of Philadelphia County housing/rental assistance. PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and the text fetch returned statewide results, not a Philadelphia-filtered list. The pilot was anchored on PA 211 items retrievable in text plus clearly-labelled secondary lists. A partial pilot is never presented here as complete.
- No "funds available", waitlist-open, or application-open status is claimed as current unless the organisation's own site said so on 2026-11-01. In particular: FreshStartPHL open status, PHA reopening dates, UESF rent-grant availability, and all four UNVERIFIED listings' funding are NOT claimed.
- No organisation is claimed to be closed or defunct. Failed or incomplete page loads were recorded as UNVERIFIED, never as closure.
- Dollar caps taken from secondary sources only (Family Promise "$1,400", ESA "$400" without same-day DHS retrieval, TFA average "$4,200") are flagged as such and must be re-checked before any send.
- Addresses and phones with logged conflicts (UESF, UCSEP, ACHIEVEability, Intercultural Family Services, Valley Youth House) are NOT settled in this report.
- No residency claim is made about the auditor. Choosing Philadelphia County for audit does not establish residency there.
- Nothing here has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation, and no organisation was contacted during this check.
