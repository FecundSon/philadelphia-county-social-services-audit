# Philadelphia County — Housing / rental assistance — pilot list

- County: Philadelphia County, PA (city and county coterminous)
- Category: Housing / rental assistance
- Date checked: 2026-11-01
- Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org), plus clearly-labelled secondary lists where PA 211 text fetch was incomplete
- Status: NOT SENT — staged only, nothing submitted to PA 211 or any organisation
- Note: This is a pilot, not a complete directory. PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven; the text fetch returned statewide results mixed across counties, not a clean Philadelphia-only list. Philadelphia items below were extracted from those pages and cross-checked against organisations' own sites where possible. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator.

## How to read this list

- "Directory claim" = what PA 211 or a secondary list said.
- "Own-site check" = what the organisation's own site (or an official city/state page) said on 2026-11-01.
- Verdicts: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED / CLARIFIED / UNVERIFIED / OUT OF SCOPE.
- No funding, waitlist or "funds available" claim is presented as current unless the organisation's own site said so on the check date.
- No organisation is called closed/defunct on the basis of a failed page load.

## In-scope pilot items (26)

### 1. City of Philadelphia — Office of Homeless Services (OHS), Prevention, Diversion and Intake (PDI)
- Address: 1401 John F. Kennedy Blvd., 10th Floor, Philadelphia, PA 19102
- Phone: Homelessness Prevention InfoLine (215) 686-7177; main (215) 686-7175
- Help: financial assistance for overdue rent or a security deposit to move into a new home; in older city material, also first/last month's rent and, in some cases, up to 12 months of rental assistance with case management
- Route: call the InfoLine and follow instructions, or visit a city-funded intake centre; email OHSPrevention@phila.gov for help applying
- Own site: https://www.phila.gov/departments/office-of-homeless-services/ (checked 2026-11-01)
- Verdict: CONFIRMED (core service). The "up to 12 months" detail comes from a 2023 city flyer, not the current OHS homepage, so it is NOT presented as a current guarantee — assistance is "based on available funding" in city wording.

### 2. Philadelphia Housing Development Corporation (PHDC) — Eviction Diversion Program / Targeted Financial Assistance (TFA)
- Address: 1234 Market St., 17th Floor, Philadelphia, PA 19107 (PHDC)
- Help: mediation plus a one-time payment to the landlord for rent arrears, plus forward rent
- Key correction: tenants do NOT apply for TFA directly. The landlord initiates Eviction Diversion; the landlord applies for TFA. Tenant income must be below 80% of Area Median Income and the tenant completes an affidavit.
- Amounts: PHDC RFP (July 29, 2026, updated Aug 19, 2026) states TFA erased arrears up to $3,500 and advanced landlords up to two months' rent; $75 million provided March 2023–December 2025, over 100,000 households supported. A city presentation cites average TFA payments of about $4,200 per household — the two figures describe different measures (cap vs. average actually paid) and must not be conflated.
- Route: via Eviction Diversion; tenant hotline via phillytenant.org; EDP tenant hotline (215) 523-9501 in older programme material
- Own/primary sources: phdcphila.org RFP; phila.gov EDP material; phillytenant.org TFA explainer
- Verdict: CONFIRMED. Eviction Diversion and TFA are ONE programme pathway, not two separate funds — do not list them twice.

### 3. PHDC — FreshStartPHL
- Help: move-in help for tenants who have been evicted or displaced and can afford ongoing rent but not move-in costs — equivalent of three months' rent to landlords (PHDC RFP: up to $4,800 in security deposits and first/last months' rent) plus up to $1,000 to tenants for moving expenses
- Launched: January 2025 (PHDC RFP); initial $19 million in state funds
- Contact: FreshStartPHL@phdc.phila.gov; PHDC (215) 448-3004 (from city resource directory)
- Verdict: CLARIFIED — programme existence and amounts confirmed from PHDC's own RFP, but whether applications are OPEN on 2026-11-01 was NOT confirmed on PHDC's own programme page in this check. Do not present as currently open.

### 7. Pennsylvania Department of Human Services — Emergency Shelter Allowance (ESA), via Philadelphia County Assistance Office
- Address: 801 Market St., Philadelphia, PA 19107
- Phone: (215) 560-7226

## Duplicates and near-duplicates flagged (do not double-count)

- PHA "Accessible Housing Information" (PA 211: 2013 Ridge Ave., (215) 684-4000) is the SAME organisation/address/phone as PHA (item 5) — a programme label, not a separate listing.
- Eviction Diversion Program = Targeted Financial Assistance pathway (item 2) — one programme, not two funds.

- VMC SSVF (item 9) + Project HOME PASSVF history (item 26) — one federal SSVF stream via a collaborative; routed once via VMC.
- Family Promise of Philadelphia (item 10) is a DIFFERENT organisation from Family Promise of Lehigh Valley (audited in the Lehigh County run) — same national network name, different affiliate, different address. Do not copy Lehigh details across.
- Catholic Social Services / Catholic Human Services (item 12) vs. Catholic Housing and Community Services (senior housing) — different services, same street address block; do not merge.
