# Philadelphia County — Substance Use Treatment / Recovery — Audit Report
**County:** Philadelphia County, PA (city and county coterminous)
**Category:** Substance use treatment / recovery
**Date checked:** 2026-11-01
**Directory:** statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
**Status: NOT SENT — staged only. Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation.**
**Method:** Public web only. For each listing, the PA 211 claim (organisation, address, phone, services, intake route, who served, hours IF stated) was recorded, then checked against the organisation's OWN website where retrievable. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. No organisation was contacted, no sign-in, no purchase, no live-browser form work.
**Limit (read first):** PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch returns a partial, statewide-mixed result set. This is a PARTIAL pilot, not a complete Philadelphia County directory. It is anchored on PA 211 items retrievable in full on 2026-11-01 plus clearly-labelled secondary lists. Do not present the 28 in-scope listings as complete, and do not send any correction that relies on an UNVERIFIED item without the pre-send re-check below.

## Headline findings

1. **No listing was called closed or defunct.** A failed or missing page load was marked UNVERIFIED, never closed.
2. **Three phone/programme mix-ups are the clearest corrections (CORRECTED):**
   - **ETHOS Treatment, Philadelphia:** aggregator/directory listings show (267) 592-3505; ETHOS's own locations pages show Philadelphia, 1528 Walnut Street, Suite 510, 19102, at **(267) 669-0300**. Use the own-site number.
   - **Gaudenzia, Spring Garden:** People With Hope **(215) 413-8260** and Philly House **(215) 849-7200** share the 1306 Spring Garden Street address but are different programmes. Philly House's own page also gives the Treatment and Referral HelpLine **(833) 976-4357**. Do not interchange the two programme numbers.
   - **JFK Behavioral Health:** the methadone-maintenance site is **907 North Broad Street, (215) 235-5520, Ext. 3612**. The main/walk-in office at **112 North Broad Street, (215) 568-0860** is a different site. Do not use the main-office number for the methadone site.
3. **Same-address, multi-programme sites are the main clarification risk:** 801 West Girard Avenue (Behavioral Wellness Center), 499 North 5th Street (Northeast Treatment Centers), 1306 Spring Garden Street (Gaudenzia), and 907 vs 112 North Broad Street (JFK) each host several programmes. A directory entry that lists only the street address without the suite/programme name is easy to misroute.
4. **Hours conflict in several places and must not be sent as settled fact:** CORA Friday hours (PA 211: Mon–Fri 8am–9pm; secondary: Fri 8am–5pm); Prevention Point hours (PA 211: Mon–Fri 8am–5pm, Sat 8am–3pm; secondary guides: Mon–Thu 8am–4pm, Fri 12pm–4pm, Sat 8am–3pm, and another: Mon–Thu 9am–5pm, Fri 12pm–5pm); Crossroads N Broad Tuesday hours are absent from its own site (Mon, Wed–Sun listed, no Tuesday hours shown). See the pre-send re-check list.
5. **Referral-only and peer-support entries must not be presented as direct treatment:** Philadelphia Mental Health Care Corporation — Behavioral Health Special Initiative (1601 Market Street) provides assessment, referral and funding support; Anchor Point (16 South 16th Street) connects people to inpatient rehab/detox programmes; SEPIA (1903 South Broad Street) provides peer support and referrals to AA groups. Labelling any of these as a treatment centre in a directory correction would be misleading.
6. **Out-of-county leakage in search results:** Bala Cynwyd (Montgomery County), Upper Darby and Glenolden (Delaware County) all appeared in Philadelphia-targeted search results. They are excluded in the appendix and must not be included in a Philadelphia County send.
7. **No bed/placement availability is reported.** Intake process only, throughout.
