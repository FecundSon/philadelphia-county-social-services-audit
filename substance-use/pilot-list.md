# Philadelphia County — Substance Use Treatment / Recovery — Pilot List
**Checked: 2026-11-01 | Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org) | Status: NOT SENT — staged only**

Philadelphia city and county are coterminous. Listings were kept only where the street address is in Philadelphia, PA. A Philadelphia postal address alone was not treated as proof of county — neighbouring-county listings found in search results are in the Out-of-Scope appendix, not the pilot count.

**Pilot scope: 28 in-scope listings + 3 out-of-scope screened listings (31 rows screened in total).**
**This is a partial pilot, not a complete directory of Philadelphia County substance-use services.** PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch returns a partial, statewide-mixed result set. The pilot is therefore anchored on PA 211 items that could be retrieved in full, plus clearly-labelled secondary lists (organisation's own site first, then city/county and reputable directories). Do not present this list as complete.
