## Headline corrections

1. **LIHEAP 2026–27 was NOT open on 2026-11-01.** Per PA DHS's own page checked 2026-11-01, the season was scheduled to open **November 2, 2026** and run through **April 23, 2027** (2025–26, Dec 3, 2025 – May 8, 2026, is closed). Any listing presenting LIHEAP as open on Nov 1, or reusing 2025–26 dates, is stale. Re-check on/after Nov 2 that the season actually opened before sending.
2. **Philadelphia has three separate utility systems:** PECO (CAP-PIPP, MEAF, Customer Relief Fund, LIURP, CARES), Philadelphia Gas Works (CRP, Crisis, Hardship, Home Comfort, CARES), and Philadelphia Water Department / Water Revenue Bureau (TAP, Senior Discount, Raise Your Hand). UESF is the cross-utility grant fund that zeroes bills with utility matching.
3. **Water Senior Discount limit corrected to $42,100/year in 2026** (phila.gov, Aug 2026), superseding the older $38,800 figure still in some PWD documents.
4. **TAP at 150% FPL is the confirmed threshold.** Council approved expanding TAP to 200% FPL (Just Water package, June 2026), but no fetched source confirms an effective date — do not publish 200% as current.
5. **Dollar Energy Fund is NOT a Philadelphia PECO/PGW route** — PA 211's own record states PECO and PPL are not participating companies. Philadelphia hardship help runs via UESF/MEAF and PGW Hardship.
6. **PGW Hardship Funds availability is contradicted** between two PGW crawls ("all grants distributed" vs "up to $1,500") — re-check before sending any availability claim.
7. **UESF street address withheld** — three conflicting Philadelphia addresses appear in sources; main phone 215-972-5170 is consistent. Publish phone + website only until re-checked.
8. **PCA Emergency Fund phone is 215-765-9000** (PA 211 record); 215-765-9040 is the PCA Helpline, not this fund. Fund: grants up to $100, age 60+, ≤175% FPL, LIHEAP/UESF first, agency/clergy referrals only.
