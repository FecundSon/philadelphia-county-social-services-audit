## Headline findings

1. **LIHEAP 2026–27 is NOT open on 2026-11-01 — it opens 2026-11-02 and closes 2027-04-23.** Verified on PA DHS's own page fetched 2026-11-01 ("scheduled to open on November 2, 2026"; 2025–26 season "NOW CLOSED") and the PA DHS 2026/27 vendor fact sheet (Nov 2, 2026 – Apr 23, 2027; crisis grants $25–$1,000 season maximum). This is the single most send-critical correction: any listing or send text that presents LIHEAP as open on Nov 1, or that reuses 2025–26 dates (Dec 3, 2025 – May 8, 2026), is wrong. Note for the send date: if the send goes out on or after Nov 2, the wording flips to "open Nov 2, 2026 – Apr 23, 2027" — the pre-send re-check must confirm the season actually opened as scheduled.
2. **Philadelphia's three-utility structure is the story.** Unlike most counties, Philadelphia has three separate utilities with three separate assistance systems: PECO (electric/gas, CAP-PIPP + MEAF + Relief Fund + LIURP + CARES), PGW (gas, CRP + Crisis + Hardship + Home Comfort + CARES), and Philadelphia Water Department/Water Revenue Bureau (TAP + Senior Discount + Raise Your Hand). UESF is the cross-utility grant fund that zeroes bills with utility matching. A send that lists only "LIHEAP" would miss most of the local system.
3. **PGW Hardship Funds availability is contradicted between two PGW crawls** — one says "All Hardship Funds Grants have been distributed at this time," the other offers "up to $1,500." Do not send an availability claim for this grant until re-checked.
4. **Water Senior Discount income limit corrected: $42,100 (2026), not $38,800.** The $38,800 figure still appears in older PWD rate documents and one PWD shut-off page; phila.gov's Aug 2026 notice states the limit "was increased to $42,100 in 2026." The limit is CPI-adjusted, so re-check at send.
5. **TAP expansion to 200% FPL is approved by Council but NOT confirmed as in effect.** City Council approved the Just Water package (June 2026) expanding TAP to 200% FPL (300% FPL structured plans), but no fetched source confirms mayoral signature or an effective date. Current confirmed TAP threshold remains 150% FPL (or Special Hardship). Do not publish 200% as current.
6. **Dollar Energy Fund is NOT a Philadelphia PECO/PGW route.** PA 211's own Dollar Energy record states "PECO and PPL are NOT participating companies." Philadelphia hardship help runs via UESF/MEAF and PGW Hardship. Sending Dollar Energy as a Philadelphia route would be an error.
7. **UESF street address cannot be published from this audit.** Three different Philadelphia addresses appear in credible sources (743 N. 24th St., 2nd Floor, 19130; 1608 Walnut St., Suite 600, 19103; 1617 JFK Blvd., Suite 490, 19103). Main phone 215-972-5170 is consistent. Publish phone + website only until the address is re-checked.
8. **PCA Emergency Fund phone corrected.** The fund itself is 215-765-9000 (PA 211 record); 215-765-9040, widely repeated by aggregators, is the PCA Helpline. The fund's cap ($100; 100 gallons oil), age 60+, ≤175% FPL, and "must first have applied to LIHEAP and/or UESF" rules are confirmed on the PA 211 record.
9. **PECO Customer Relief Fund: $750 credit, but funds-remaining UNVERIFIED.** The 2026 expansion ($750, past-due $1–$2,500, 0–150% and 151–300% FPL) is well documented, but whether money remains on the send date is not established. The earlier $500 launch figure is a prior phase, not the current figure.
10. **NEC addresses need per-site re-checks.** The 16-centre NEC network (ECA-managed) is confirmed, and NKCDC is confirmed at PA 211 + own-site level. Other sampled NECs rest on a March 2026 PECO brochure, with two street-address conflicts (CONCILIO 141 vs 41 E. Hunting Park; ACHIEVEability 59 N. 60th St. vs 5901 Market St. Suite 410).

## What this report does NOT claim

- This is a **pilot, not a complete directory** of Philadelphia utilities assistance. The 16 NECs are sampled, not enumerated; several PECO/PGW/WRB sub-programmes (e.g. budget billing, payment plans generally) are mentioned only where they attach to a listed programme.
- PA 211's county-filtered Philadelphia utilities list was **not** fully retrieved: fetched PA 211 search pages are statewide, paginated mixes (only NKCDC and PCA surfaced as Philadelphia-address entries on the fetched pages). A live, county-filtered PA 211 check is required before any send, and may add or correct items.
- **No organisation is described as closed or defunct.** PGW Hardship's "distributed" notice is treated as a funds-availability question, not a closure. No failed page load was treated as evidence of closure.
- Benefit amounts are **season- and funds-dependent** and are reported as checked on 2026-11-01, not as guarantees. LIHEAP cash $200–$1,000 is the 2025–26 range on the current PA DHS page; the 2026–27 cash range was not separately confirmed on the official page on 2026-11-01.
- No claim is made about any person's residence. The auditor is not described as a Philadelphia County resident anywhere in this audit.
- Hours, walk-in availability, and current fund balances were mostly not verified on 2026-11-01 and are flagged below rather than asserted.
