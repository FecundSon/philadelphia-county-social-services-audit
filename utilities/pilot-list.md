# Philadelphia County, PA — Utilities Assistance: Pilot List

- **County:** Philadelphia County, PA (city and county are coterminous)
- **Category:** Utilities assistance — LIHEAP, shut-off help, fuel funds
- **Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
- **Date checked:** 2026-11-01
- **Status:** NOT SENT — staged only, for audit purposes. Nothing in this file has been sent to PA 211 or any organisation.
- **Pilot scope:** 26 in-scope Philadelphia County listings (U1–U26), plus 4 out-of-scope / non-Philadelphia records checked and excluded (X1–X4). This is a pilot, not a complete directory of Philadelphia utilities help.
- **Limit disclosed:** PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch returns a statewide, paginated mix without a county filter in the fetched text. Philadelphia items were therefore anchored on PA 211 records that carry an exact Philadelphia address, plus the utilities' own sites and City of Philadelphia pages. A live PA 211 county-filtered check is still needed before any send (see pre-send re-check list in the audit report).

## Key date-stamped window — read first

- **PA LIHEAP 2026–27: NOT OPEN on 2026-11-01.** Official PA DHS page checked 2026-11-01 states: "The 2026-2027 LIHEAP Season is scheduled to open on November 2, 2026" and "The 2025-2026 LIHEAP SEASON IS NOW CLOSED" (2025–26 ran Dec 3, 2025 – May 8, 2026). Closing date for 2026–27: **April 23, 2027** (PA DHS vendor fact sheet; York County / Duquesne Light / National Fuel pages corroborate). Any directory listing that presents LIHEAP as currently open on 2026-11-01 is stale for that day.
- **LIHEAP hotline:** 1-866-857-7095 (PA DHS, checked 2026-11-01). **COMPASS:** compass.state.pa.us / dhs.pa.gov/COMPASS.
- **LIHEAP income limits on the PA DHS page (effective Feb 1, 2026):** 1 person $23,940; 2 $32,460; 3 $40,980; 4 $49,500; 5 $58,020; 6 $66,540; 7 $75,060; 8 $83,580; 9 $92,100; 10 $100,620; +$8,520 per additional person. Cash grants $200–$1,000 (2025–26 wording on the page); crisis grants $25 up to a $1,000 season maximum (2026–27 vendor fact sheet).
