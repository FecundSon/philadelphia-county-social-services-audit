## Other key clarifications

- Do not quote 9 a.m.–5 p.m. office hours as intake hours: intake windows for Community Legal Services, Philadelphia Legal Assistance, SeniorLAW, Legal Clinic for the Disabled, HIAS Pennsylvania and Pennsylvania Health Law Project are narrower, topic-specific, and in several cases phone-only.
- Do not present Philadelphia VIP, Public Interest Law Center, HIAS Pennsylvania, Legal Clinic for the Disabled or Juvenile Law Center as general walk-in clinics — each has a referral, appointment, no-walk-in, or impact-case limit.
- Statewide organisations with a Philadelphia office are labelled statewide in the full report, with the statewide route first where the organisation states one (for example, Pennsylvania Health Law Project Helpline 1-800-274-3258; SeniorLAW HelpLine 1-877-727-7529).
- Two recurring aggregator listings are OUT OF SCOPE for Philadelphia County and must not be sent as Philadelphia listings: Legal Aid of Southeastern Pennsylvania (serves Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Montgomery counties) and Community Justice Project (states on its own site that it does not handle Philadelphia-area cases).
