# Philadelphia County — Legal Aid: Pilot List

- County: Philadelphia County, PA (city and county are coterminous)
- Category: Legal aid
- Date compiled: 2026-11-01
- Directory anchor: Statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
- Status: NOT SENT — staged only, for later approval before any contact with PA 211 or any organisation
- Scope note: This is a pilot, NOT a complete list of Philadelphia County legal aid. PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and a text fetch does not return a complete county list, so this pilot anchors on PA 211 items that could be retrieved plus clearly-labelled secondary lists (Pennsylvania Legal Aid Network, PALawHELP, Justia county pages, LegalAidOffices, Free Library of Philadelphia Community Resource Guide). See the audit report for the limit disclosure.
- Method note: Contact information only. No legal advice is rewritten or offered here. Own organisation website beats directory; directory beats aggregator. A failed page load was never treated as a closure.
- County rule applied: A Philadelphia postal address alone did not settle county. Exact street addresses were used, and neighbouring-county listings were excluded or marked OUT OF SCOPE.
