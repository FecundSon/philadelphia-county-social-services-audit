# Philadelphia County, PA — Legal Aid Audit Report

- County: Philadelphia County, PA (city and county are coterminous)
- Category: Legal aid
- Date checked: 2026-11-01
- Directory: Statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
- Status: NOT SENT — staged only. Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation, and no send may go without separate approval of its exact text and route.
- Auditor note: This audit covers CONTACT INFORMATION ONLY. It does not rewrite, summarise or offer legal advice, and it records no information about any individual client.

## Method

1. Started from statewide PA 211 search result sets for General Legal Aid, Legal Counseling and Lawyer Referral Services, plus PA 211 programme records that could be retrieved.
2. For each candidate, checked the organisation's OWN website where retrievable. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator (Justia, LegalAidOffices, PALawHELP, PLAN, Free Library guide).
3. Applied the county rule strictly: Philadelphia city/county are coterminous, but a Philadelphia postal address alone did not settle county — exact street addresses were used, neighbouring-county listings were excluded, and statewide services presented as Philadelphia offices were labelled statewide rather than given an invented local office.
4. Recorded, per listing: directory claim, own-site check, areas of law if stated, eligibility (income, county, case type), intake route, and walk-in vs appointment. Verdicts: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED / CLARIFIED / UNVERIFIED / OUT OF SCOPE.
5. A failed page load was never treated as a closure. Items that could not be verified are marked UNVERIFIED, not removed.
6. No organisation was contacted, and no form was submitted.

## Limit — read this before using the counts

PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and a text fetch does not return a complete Philadelphia County list. The PA 211 result sets retrieved for this audit were partial (for example, Legal Counseling returned a statewide set in which only some records were Philadelphia records). This pilot therefore anchors on PA 211 items that could be retrieved plus clearly-labelled secondary lists (Pennsylvania Legal Aid Network Philadelphia Region page, PALawHELP, Justia Philadelphia County pages, LegalAidOffices Philadelphia County page, Free Library of Philadelphia Community Resource Guide). **This pilot is NOT a complete list of Philadelphia County legal aid, and must never be presented as one.** A pre-send re-check should try a live PA 211 search to confirm whether further Philadelphia legal-aid records exist beyond these 28 items.

## Headline findings

- 28 pilot items: 11 CONFIRMED, 5 CORRECTED, 8 CLARIFIED, 2 UNVERIFIED, 2 OUT OF SCOPE.
- The two largest Philadelphia general legal-aid providers are confirmed and distinct: Community Legal Services of Philadelphia (1424 Chestnut St, 215-981-3700; and 1410 W. Erie Ave) and Philadelphia Legal Assistance (718 Arch St, Suite 300N, 215-981-3800). They must not be merged, and their topic-specific hotlines must not be collapsed into one number.
- Five address/phone corrections are material enough to send (once approved and re-checked):
  1. Public Interest Law Center moved from 125 S. 9th St, Suite 700 to 1617 JFK Blvd, Suite 1650, Philadelphia, PA 19103 (phone unchanged, 215-627-7100; no walk-ins).
  2. Justice at Work Pennsylvania (formerly Friends of Farmworkers) moved from 699 Ranstead St to 990 Spring Garden St, Suite 300, Philadelphia, PA 19123 (phone unchanged, 215-733-0878).
  3. Philadelphia VIP moved from 1500 Walnut St, Suite 400 to 1500 JFK Blvd, Suite 1850, Philadelphia, PA 19102 (phone unchanged, 215-523-9550), and most individual cases reach VIP by referral via CLS/PLA, not by direct walk-in.
  4. Legal Clinic for the Disabled: own-site phone is 215-587-3158, not 215-587-3350 as shown in several aggregators; new clients by phone only, no walk-ins; housing intake line shown as closed until further notice.
  5. Philadelphia Bar Association LRIS: own site gives 1101 Market St, 10th Floor (PA 211 shows 11th Floor); phone 215-238-6333 confirmed — and LRIS is a paid-attorney referral service, not free legal aid, which is the more important correction.
- The most common error class in secondary sources is the suite number (see also PILP Suite 304 South, PHLP Suite 404, and the unresolved DRP Suite 400 vs 500).
- The most common presentation trap is office hours vs intake hours: 9 a.m.–5 p.m. office hours appear for CLS, PLA, SeniorLAW, LCD, HIAS and PHLP, but none of those is the intake window. Intake windows are narrower, topic-specific, and in several cases phone-only.
- Five listings must not be presented as general walk-in clinics: Philadelphia VIP (referral route), Public Interest Law Center (no walk-ins), HIAS Pennsylvania (phone-only intake, no walk-ins), Legal Clinic for the Disabled (phone-only new clients), and Juvenile Law Center (impact firm, not a general intake). Two more are referral/narrow-scope only in practice: Support Center for Child Advocates (children in DHS/court systems) and Pennsylvania Institutional Law Project (mail intake for institutionalised people).
- Statewide organisations with a Philadelphia office must be labelled statewide in any send, with the statewide route first where the organisation states one: Pennsylvania Health Law Project (Helpline 1-800-274-3258), Pennsylvania Institutional Law Project (mail intake), SeniorLAW HelpLine (1-877-727-7529, all 67 counties), Disability Rights Pennsylvania (toll-free intake 1-800-692-7443), Education Law Center, Women's Law Project, Pennsylvania Client Assistance Program, and ACLU of Pennsylvania.
- Two recurring aggregator listings are OUT OF SCOPE and should not be sent as Philadelphia listings: Legal Aid of Southeastern Pennsylvania (serves Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Montgomery counties) and Community Justice Project (states on its own site that it does not handle Philadelphia-area cases).

## What this report does NOT claim

- It does NOT claim to be a complete list of Philadelphia County legal aid. The PA 211 text retrieval was partial (see Limit above); other Philadelphia legal-aid records may exist in PA 211 that this pilot did not retrieve.
- It does NOT claim that any organisation is closed, defunct or no longer operating. UNVERIFIED means only that a current own-site confirmation was not retrieved on 2026-11-01.
- It does NOT claim current intake hours where sources conflict or are old. Where the report says "re-check," the hours in the pilot list are the best evidence found, not a verified current timetable.
- It does NOT offer legal advice, assess any person's eligibility for any service, or record information about any individual client. Eligibility notes are copied from directory/own-site descriptions only.
- It does NOT claim that a Philadelphia postal address proves Philadelphia County residence or service: county was checked by exact street address, and neighbouring-county services were excluded even where aggregators list them under "Philadelphia area."
- It does NOT claim that statewide organisations' Philadelphia offices are walk-in general intakes. Several statewide routes are helpline, mail or phone-only by the organisation's own statement.
- It does NOT claim that fees, income thresholds or consultation charges are current where sources conflict (notably LRIS consultation fee and HIAS sliding-scale threshold). Those figures are flagged, not quoted as fact, for any send.
