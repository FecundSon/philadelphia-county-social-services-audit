# Philadelphia County — Disability services: pilot list

**County:** Philadelphia County, PA (city and county are coterminous).
**Date checked:** 2026-11-01.
**Status:** NOT SENT. Staged only. Nothing in this file has been submitted to PA 211 or any organisation.
**Directory anchor:** Statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org). PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch may be incomplete; where a PA 211 record could not be retrieved in full, the listing below is anchored on the PA 211 item that *could* be retrieved plus a clearly-labelled secondary list or the organisation's own site. This is a partial pilot, not a complete county directory.
**County rule applied:** A Philadelphia postal address alone does not settle county. Exact addresses were used; listings in Montgomery, Delaware, Bucks, Chester, Camden and other neighbouring counties are listed separately as OUT OF SCOPE. Statewide-only services are labelled statewide; no local office has been invented for them.
**Auditor note:** The auditor is not described as a local resident of Philadelphia County.
