# Philadelphia County, PA — Disability services audit

- **County:** Philadelphia County, PA (city and county coterminous)
- **Category:** Disability services
- **Date checked:** 2026-11-01
- **Method:** Public web only — web search and page-text fetch. No live browser tasks, no sign-ins, no purchases, no contact with any organisation. Directory claim checked against the organisation's own website where possible. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. A failed or incomplete page load was never treated as closure.
- **Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org), plus clearly-labelled secondary lists and own sites where PA 211 text was incomplete.
- **Status: NOT SENT.** Staged only. Nothing in this report has been submitted to PA 211 or to any organisation.

## Headline findings

- **Reviewed: 33 listings. In-scope pilot: 28. Out of scope / statewide-only: 5.**
- **Verdicts (in-scope 28): CONFIRMED 12, CORRECTED 4, CLARIFIED 9, UNVERIFIED 3.**
- **Verdicts (out of scope 5): all OUT OF SCOPE, listed separately below and not counted in the 28.**
- This is a **partial pilot, not a complete Philadelphia County disability directory**. PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and returned partial, statewide-mixed results in text fetch; the pilot was built from PA 211 items that could be retrieved plus labelled secondary lists and own sites, filtered by exact address.

### Headline corrections (the items a PA 211 send would actually change)

1. **City Office for People with Disabilities — address.** Older listings show City Hall (Room 260D, and Room 114 on the accessibility-policy page). The current phila.gov office page gives Municipal Services Building, 1401 John F. Kennedy Blvd, 1st Floor, Philadelphia, PA 19102, phone (215) 686-2798, email ada.request@phila.gov. Any PA 211 record still showing only a City Hall address should be updated to the MSB address.
2. **Art-Reach — suite.** Own site: 1315 Walnut St, Suite 1017, Philadelphia, PA 19107, (267) 515-6720, info@art-reach.org. A scholarship PDF shows Suite 1417 at the same address and an older aggregator shows 1501 Cherry St. Use Suite 1017; do not use 1417 or the Cherry St address as the current office.
3. **SEPTA Access — phone.** Use Access Customer Service (215) 580-7145 as the headline paratransit contact (general SEPTA (215) 580-7800; TDD/TTY (215) 580-7853). A Visit Philadelphia guide also lists (215) 580-7720 for trip scheduling; that number is unverified as a scheduling line and should not be published as the headline number without re-check.
4. **Vision for Equality — phone.** PA 211's parent-groups record shows (215) 303-3501; the organisation's main/directory phone is (215) 923-3349 at 718 Arch St, Suite 6N, Philadelphia, PA 19106. Do not present the PA 211 number as the organisation's main number until it is confirmed as a current programme line; the main number is the safe headline contact, with the PA 211 number flagged for re-check.
5. **County errors to remove, not just correct.** Elwyn's headquarters (111 Elwyn Rd, Elwyn, PA 19063) is in Delaware County, not Philadelphia County, and PATF (1004 W 9th Ave, King of Prussia, PA 19406) is in Montgomery County and is a statewide service. Neither should appear as a Philadelphia County local office. Likewise, the Norristown OVR office (Montgomery County) is distinct from the Philadelphia OVR office.
6. **Do not publish an Inglis House bed count.** Secondary sources disagree (152 / 252 / 297). No bed count is asserted in this report.
7. **Regional, not Philadelphia-only, remits — label, don't shrink.** Liberty Resources serves Philadelphia, Chester, Delaware and Montgomery counties; TechOWL's Philadelphia ATRC serves five counties; SpArc/PersonLink/Legal Clinic/Art-Reach/SEPTA Access all serve a wider region from a Philadelphia base. Their Philadelphia addresses are real; their service areas are wider, and a send should say so.

## What this report does NOT claim

- It is **not** a complete list of Philadelphia County disability services. It is a 28-item pilot plus 5 out-of-scope examples, built from retrievable PA 211 items and labelled secondary lists.
- It does **not** claim any organisation is closed or defunct. No organisation is called closed/defunct in this report; UNVERIFIED means "not confirmed in this check", not "gone".
- It does **not** claim hours, eligibility, fees or service details beyond what is cited per listing. Where a detail was not retrieved, it is marked "not stated" or left to the pre-send re-check list, not guessed.
- It does **not** claim a Philadelphia postal address proves Philadelphia County. Every in-scope listing was checked by exact address; neighbouring-county listings were excluded on that basis.
- It does **not** present statewide or regional services as Philadelphia-only. Regional remits (Liberty Resources, TechOWL, SpArc, PersonLink, Legal Clinic, Art-Reach, SEPTA Access) are labelled as such.
- It does **not** describe the auditor as a local resident of Philadelphia County.
- Nothing here has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation, and no organisation was contacted.
