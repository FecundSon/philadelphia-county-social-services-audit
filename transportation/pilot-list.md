# Philadelphia County — Transportation pilot list
Date checked: 2026-11-01. Status: NOT SENT — staged only.
Method: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org) as directory anchor, checked against each organisation's OWN website where possible. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch may be incomplete — this pilot is therefore anchored on PA 211 items actually retrieved plus clearly-labelled secondary lists, and is NOT presented as a complete Philadelphia County transportation list.
Geography rule: Philadelphia city and county are coterminous. Listings in Montgomery, Delaware, Bucks, Chester, Camden and other neighbouring counties are OUT OF SCOPE even if they serve Philadelphia as a destination. A Philadelphia postal address alone does not settle county — exact addresses were used.
Fare/schedule rule: every fare and schedule below is date-stamped 2026-11-01 and must not be presented as permanent; routes, fares and contracts change.

## What was NOT included

- No listing was called closed/defunct from a failed page load. Failed or incomplete fetches are marked UNVERIFIED.
- No organisation was contacted. No submission was made to PA 211 or anyone else.
- Taxi / rideshare firms, intercity bus carriers and airport services were excluded as general commercial transport, not social-services transport, except PHLASH and Indego where they function as subsidised/access programmes.
