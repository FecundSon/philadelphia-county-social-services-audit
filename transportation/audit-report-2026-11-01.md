# Philadelphia County, PA — Transportation audit
Date checked: 2026-11-01. Status: NOT SENT — staged only. Nothing in this report has been submitted to PA 211 or to any organisation.
County: Philadelphia County, PA (city and county coterminous). Category: Transportation.
Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org).
Method: for each listing, directory claim recorded and checked against the organisation's OWN website where possible. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. Public web only (browser.search + browser.open page-text fetch). No live browser tasks, no sign-ins, no purchases, no contact with any organisation.
Auditor note: the auditor is not described as a local resident of Philadelphia County.

## Headline findings

- 27 listings audited: 10 CONFIRMED, 3 CORRECTED, 10 CLARIFIED, 1 UNVERIFIED, 3 OUT OF SCOPE.
- Correction 1 — SEPTA paratransit fare is $5.75, not $4.25 (2026-11-01). Older flyers, JEVS and mobility guides still show $4.25 for ADA/Shared-Ride. SEPTA's own ADA page (fetched 2026-11-01) states $5.75 one-way within the service region, and the September 2025 tariff change raised ADA/Shared-Ride from $4.25 to $5.75 and removed the 75-cent county surcharge. Any PA 211 entry still showing $4.25 is stale.
- Correction 2 — Philadelphia County MATP broker branding: LogistiCare → Modivcare. Community Behavioral Health's own MATP page (fetched 2026-11-01) directs riders to "call Modivcare" and gives problem line 1-877-835-7420. Older records showing only LogistiCare and 1-877-835-7412 should be updated, and the current scheduling number re-verified before any send.
- Correction 3 — Pennsylvania Prison Society bus scope: PA 211's older description ("all state correctional facilities except Camp Hill, Chester, and Graterford") is outdated. The Society's own site (2026) lists nine Philadelphia departures: SCI Benner, Frackville, Greene, Huntingdon, Laurel Highlands, Mahanoy, Muncy, Smithfield and Somerset, at $20 round trip, children under 18 free.
- Clarification (systemic) — SEPTA Access is NOT medical transport. SEPTA's own site states this explicitly ("not a taxi service, medical transport or 'special care' service"). MATP, by contrast, is medical-only (Medical Assistance-covered services) and is NOT general public transport. Any directory entry blurring these two — medical transport listed as general transport or vice versa — is wrong.
- Clarification (systemic) — "CCT Connect" is the former name. The current name is SEPTA Access. Entries still filed only under CCT Connect should be cross-referenced to SEPTA Access.
- Clarification — City Zero Fare is NOT open for new enrolment at 2026-11-01 (City page, 2026-10-13: lottery/auto-enrolment, ~90% lottery + ~10% via community organisations, extended FY2026 and FY2027, "not currently enrolling new participants"). It must not be listed as "apply here."
- Clarification — PCA is a referral/coordinating agency for senior transport in this check, not an evidenced direct ride fleet; Liberty Resources provides information/referral and travel training, not rides; KleinLife lists "transportation assistance" with no fare or booking terms retrieved.
- Not operating yet — Vets Ride 4 Free states on its own site that it is "Coming in 2027" and "not providing rides yet." It must not be listed as a current ride service.
- Out of scope — Community Transit (Delaware County, trips must originate there), Visiting Angels Havertown (Delaware County base) and TransNet/Suburban Transit MATP (Montgomery County) were excluded despite Philadelphia destinations or a Philadelphia service mention. County is determined by exact address/origin rule, not by a Philadelphia postal address or destination alone.

## What this report does NOT claim

- This is NOT a complete list of Philadelphia County transportation services. PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch returned snippets, not complete per-record detail; the pilot is anchored on PA 211 items actually retrieved plus clearly-labelled secondary lists.
- No fare, schedule or route in this report is permanent. All are date-stamped 2026-11-01; SEPTA fares changed in September 2025 and service patterns changed through 2025–2026 cuts/restorations, so every fare must be re-checked before any send or publication.
- No organisation was called closed or defunct. A failed page load or an unretrieved detail is marked UNVERIFIED, never treated as closure.
- No organisation was contacted, and nothing was sent to PA 211 or anyone else. This audit is staged only.
- The auditor is not described as a local resident of Philadelphia County, and no residency is claimed or implied.
- Secondary sources (Wikipedia, Jawnt, Visit Philly, school pages, Idealist, mobility guides) are labelled as secondary wherever used; they do not outrank an organisation's own site.
