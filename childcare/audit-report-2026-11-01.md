# Philadelphia County, PA — Child care / family support audit
**County:** Philadelphia County, PA (city and county coterminous)
**Category:** Child care / family support
**Date checked:** 2026-11-01
**Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
**Method:** Public web only — browser.search and browser.open text fetch. No live browser tasks, no sign-ins, no purchases, no contact with any organisation. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. For each listing: directory claim vs organisation's own website where retrievable.
**Status: NOT SENT.** This audit is staged only. Nothing has been submitted to PA 211 or to any organisation. No send may go without separate approval of its exact text and route.

## Headline findings
- **29 listings in the pilot: 26 in-scope, 3 out of scope.**
- **Verdicts (in-scope, n=26): CONFIRMED 14, CORRECTED 2, CLARIFIED 9, UNVERIFIED 1.**
- **Headline correction 1 — Miss Marty's moved site.** Aggregator records still circulate the old 226 W Harvey Street site (with old phone 215-844-8083, shown as "temporarily closed") and a wrong phone 215-843-3830. The current own-site address is **6001 Germantown Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19144, 215-843-8299**. Any PA 211 record still pointing at Harvey Street should be corrected to Germantown Avenue — the school did not close, it moved a few blocks.
- **Headline correction 2 — Turning Points main office.** Directory records mix three Philadelphia addresses (415 S. 15th Street; 4601 Market Street; 4329 Griscom Street). The main office is **415 S. 15th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19146, 215-875-8200**; the others are separate programme sites and must not be merged into a single listing.
- **Headline clarification — "Philadelphia" region pages include other counties.** KinderCare's Philadelphia region page lists Bala Cynwyd (Montgomery County) and Folcroft (Delaware County), and the YMCA PHLpreK page footer lists a Conshohocken (Montgomery County) administrative address. All three are OUT OF SCOPE here despite appearing on Philadelphia-labelled pages. A Philadelphia postal address, or a Philadelphia-labelled search page, does not settle county — exact address does.
- **Headline gap — Brightside 40th & Market.** Aggregators list Brightside Academy at **4011 Market Street, Philadelphia, PA 19104, 215-386-0910**, but Brightside's own locations page retrieved 2026-11-01 does not list 4011 Market Street (its nearest own listing is 4047-51 Lancaster Avenue, "40th & Lancaster"). This is marked UNVERIFIED, not closed: the centre may have moved, been renamed, or simply be omitted from the own-site list. Re-check before any send; do not publish it as closed.
- **Headline gap — PA 211 completeness.** PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch did not return a complete Philadelphia child-care list. Only two PA 211 records were retrieved in full (Congreso ELECT and Congreso Harcum, both confirmed). The rest of this pilot is anchored on providers' own sites and City pages, with aggregator records flagged as such. **This report is a partial pilot, not a complete county directory, and must not be presented as one.**
- **Subsidy routes verified at system level, not per-centre waitlists.** PHLpreK is free for Philadelphia-resident 3- and 4-year-olds with no income or employment requirement (City of Philadelphia + YMCA own pages). Child Care Works / ELRC subsidy: helpline 888-461-5437; PHLpreK help 844-745-7735. No waitlist status was stated on any own site retrieved, so none is claimed. The ELRC income table found online is dated 2019 and must not be republished — see re-check list. The City PHLpreK page text as retrieved still cites the 2025-2026 cycle / January 20, 2026 opening — re-check the current school-year cycle before any send.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It is NOT a complete directory of Philadelphia County child care / family support. PA 211's full Philadelphia list was not retrievable by text fetch, and this pilot covers 29 items only.
- It does NOT claim any centre is closed or defunct. No failed page load was treated as closure; the one doubtful centre (Brightside 4011 Market Street) is UNVERIFIED, not closed.
- It does NOT claim waitlist status, current vacancies, fees (except PHLpreK free and Congreso ELECT free, as stated in the sources cited), or summer vs school-year hours for any centre, except where the table explicitly says so.
- It does NOT publish any information about individual children, and no such information was collected.
- It does NOT claim current ELRC income thresholds (the only table found was dated 2019) or the current PHLpreK application cycle (City page wording as retrieved cites 2025-2026 / January 20, 2026).
- It does NOT describe the auditor as a local resident of Philadelphia County.
- It does NOT claim that aggregator hours, where flagged as aggregator-sourced, have been confirmed on the provider's own site.
- Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or to any provider, and no provider was contacted.
