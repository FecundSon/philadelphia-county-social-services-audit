# Philadelphia County, PA — Employment / Job Help Audit
Date checked: 2026-11-01
Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
Method: public web search and page-text fetch only. No live browser tasks, no
sign-ins, no purchases, no contact with any organisation. Own site beats
directory; directory beats aggregator.
Status: NOT SENT. Nothing in this report has been submitted to PA 211 or to any
organisation. This audit is staged only.

## Headline findings

1. **PA CareerLink West has moved — PA 211 still shows the old address.**
   PA 211 lists the West centre at 3901 Market St, Philadelphia, PA 19104.
   The CareerLink own-site contact page (fetched 2026-11-01) lists West at
   5548 Chestnut St, Suite 101, Philadelphia, PA 19139, with the same phone
   (215) 473-3630. Same phone + new address = a move, not a second centre.
   Anyone following the PA 211 address would go to the wrong building.
2. **A phone-number mix-up between CareerLink sites.** A PA 211 snippet
   attaches (267) 792-3835 to the Northwest (Germantown Ave) record. On the
   own site, (267) 792-3835 is the Suburban Station TTY number; Northwest
   voice is (215) 987-6503. Publishing the PA 211 snippet number as a
   Northwest voice number would misdirect callers.
3. **The Welcoming Center has moved.** Older lists (CAP4Kids, city pages) show
   1617 JFK Blvd, Suite 555. Its own site now shows 211 N. 13th St, 4th Floor,
   Philadelphia, PA 19107, same phone (215) 557-2626.
4. **Three layers, one system — do not triple-count.** Philadelphia Works is
   the workforce board/funder, EDSI operates system-wide services inside the
   centres, and the four PA CareerLink centres are the public-facing sites.
   The same applies inside organisations: OIC has three programme rows at one
   address, JEVS/Orleans share one campus, ACHIEVEability has two programmes
   at one address. Programme rows are kept, but they are not separate providers.
5. **No organisation is called closed or defunct in this report.** Where an
   own site could not be checked, the item is UNVERIFIED or CLARIFIED, never
   guessed — including MenzFit, where an aggregator tax page mentions a 2020
   IRS revocation entry that was not verified against IRS records and plays
   no part in any verdict here.

## What this report does NOT claim

- This is NOT a complete census of Philadelphia County employment services.
  PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch may be incomplete;
  the pilot is anchored on PA 211 items actually retrieved plus clearly-labelled
  secondary lists. Other Philadelphia providers (for example additional
  neighbourhood workforce programmes) may exist and are not covered here.
- It does NOT claim any organisation is closed or defunct. No failed page load
  was treated as evidence of closure.
- It does NOT claim the auditor is a Philadelphia County resident, and it does
  not present any listing as personally visited or phoned.
- Hours, eligibility wording and phone numbers marked CLARIFIED or UNVERIFIED
  must not be published as fact until re-checked (see below).
- Programme-level rows under one organisation (OIC, JEVS, ACHIEVEability) are
  not separate providers and must not be counted as such in any send.
