# Philadelphia County — Employment / Job Help — Pilot List
Checked 2026-11-01. Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org).
Status: NOT SENT. Staged only; nothing has been submitted to PA 211 or any organisation.

Philadelphia city and county are coterminous. Listings in Montgomery, Delaware,
Bucks, Chester, Camden and other neighbouring counties are OUT OF SCOPE and are
listed separately at the end, not counted as Philadelphia services. A Philadelphia
postal address alone was not treated as proof of county; exact addresses were used.

PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch may be incomplete. This
pilot is therefore anchored on PA 211 items actually retrieved (search-result
snippets and PA 211 record pages surfaced by search) plus clearly-labelled
secondary lists (CAP4Kids Philadelphia employment page, RAD Consulting training
list, city and provider sites). It is a partial pilot, NOT a complete census of
Philadelphia employment services. Own site beats directory; directory beats
aggregator.

## Duplicate / funder-name flags (do not double-count)

- PA CareerLink centres (#1–#4), EDSI (#5) and Philadelphia Works (#6) are one
  system: Philadelphia Works is the board/funder, EDSI is the system-wide services
  operator inside the centres, and the centres are the public-facing sites. Counting
  all three layers as separate "providers" would triple-count the same service.
- OIC #7, #8 and #9 are one organisation at one address; they are programme-level
  rows, not three providers.
- JEVS #11, #12 and #13: Project WoW and Orleans Tech share the 2770 Red Lion Rd
  campus and JEVS as parent; they are distinct programmes, not distinct organisations.
- ACHIEVEability #18 and #19 are one organisation at one address with two programmes.
- The PA 211 record "PA CareerLink — Career Development Philadelphia County"
  (veterans services, 1617 JFK Blvd) is the same Suburban Station centre as #1, not
  a fifth centre; the current own-site contact page lists exactly four centres.
