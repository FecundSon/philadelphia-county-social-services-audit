## Method and limits

Public web search and page-text fetch only; no contact with any organisation.
Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. PA 211 search pages are
JavaScript-driven and text fetch may be incomplete, so this pilot is anchored
on PA 211 items actually retrieved plus clearly-labelled secondary lists. It
is a partial pilot, NOT a complete census of Philadelphia employment services.
No organisation is called closed or defunct in this audit; items that could
not be confirmed from an own site are marked UNVERIFIED or CLARIFIED, never
guessed.
