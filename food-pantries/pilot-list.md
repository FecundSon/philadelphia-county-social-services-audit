# Philadelphia County, PA — Food Pantries: Pilot List

**Checked: 2026-11-01. Status: staged only. Nothing has been sent to statewide PA 211 or anyone.**
**Directory anchor: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org).**

## Important limit — read first

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven. A text fetch did not return a
complete, extractable Philadelphia County food-pantry result list on
2026-11-01. One PA 211 search result was retrieved directly in this
audit: Broad Street Love / Broad Street Ministry, 315 South Broad Street
(meals only — see item 9). The pilot below is therefore anchored on that
PA 211 item plus clearly-labelled secondary lists, principally the
needhelppayingbills.com Philadelphia page (fetched in full 2026-11-01),
the Share Food Program Philadelphia County site list (July 2025, via the
PA House PDF), Philabundance's own FAQ, and Lemontree/foodpantries.org
secondary listings. **This pilot is NOT a complete list of Philadelphia
County pantries, and must not be presented as one.** A live-browser check
of search.pa211.org for Philadelphia County is still outstanding before
any send to PA 211.

Philadelphia city and county are coterminous. Listings in Montgomery,
Delaware, Bucks, Chester, Camden and other neighbouring counties are
OUT OF SCOPE even if they serve Philadelphia-area residents. A
Philadelphia postal address alone was not treated as proof of county;
exact street addresses were used.

The auditor is NOT a local resident of Philadelphia County. This audit
was prepared by county-by-county checking of public web sources only.
No organisation was contacted.
