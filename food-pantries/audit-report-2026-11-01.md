# Philadelphia County, PA — Food Pantry Listing Audit

**County:** Philadelphia County, PA (City of Philadelphia — city and
county are coterminous)
**Category:** Food pantries
**Checked:** 2026-11-01
**Method:** Public web search and page-text fetch only. No organisation,
PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
**Status: NOT SENT. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide
PA 211 or anyone. This audit is staged only.**
**Auditor note:** The auditor is NOT a local resident of Philadelphia
County. This audit was prepared by county-by-county checking of public
web sources.

## Headline findings (only own-site-verified material corrections)

1. **Bebashi — address corrected.** The directory address 1217 Spring
   Garden St is superseded by Bebashi's own current address, **1237
   Spring Garden St, Philadelphia, PA 19123, (215) 769-3561**. Office
   hours (Mon–Fri 9am–5pm) must not be published as pantry hours.
2. **Saint Mark's Food Cupboard — days corrected (own site).** The
   cupboard operates **Thursdays and Fridays only, 9:30–11am**,
   1625 Locust St, Philadelphia, PA 19103, (215) 735-1416. Secondary
   listings claiming Tuesday–Friday are wrong on own-site evidence.
   The Saturday Soup Bowl (6–8am) is a separate meal, not the cupboard.
3. **Katie's Cupboard @ Tabor — confirmed (own site).** Every Thursday
   10am–noon, back door of the church, 4860 N Howard St, Philadelphia,
   PA 19120, (215) 455-1706, for anyone experiencing food insecurity.
4. **North10 Lenfest / 10th St. Market — hours corrected.** Pantry
   **Tuesdays and Thursdays 10am–12pm** (Share Food Program July 2025
   list, corroborated by Lemontree), 3890 N 10th St, Philadelphia, PA
   19140, (267) 908-9000 — NOT 1–3pm as a place listing claims. Centre
   hours (8am–8pm) are not pantry hours.
5. **Broad Street Ministry is meals only, not a grocery pantry.**
   PA 211 itself files it as "Emergency Kitchen (Meals Only)",
   315 S Broad St, Philadelphia, PA 19107, (215) 735-4847, lunch
   Mon–Fri 11:30am–1:15pm. Filing it as a food pantry without that
   qualifier misleads.
6. **Philabundance warehouses are NOT walk-in pantries.** Philabundance's
   own FAQ states it does not distribute food to the public directly
   from its warehouses or Community Kitchen. The genuine public
   choice-model pantry is the Community Food Center at/near
   601 N Lehigh Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19133; its current days/hours
   were not verified on Philabundance's own site in this check.
7. **Crossroads is NOT reported as closed.** A secondary aggregator
   labels Crossroads Community Center "[Closed]", but its own pantry
   site still describes a Philabundance distribution pantry (minimum
   100 food boxes monthly) with no hours stated. Current operation is
   UNVERIFIED — that is a gap, not a closure finding, and must not be
   published as "closed".
8. **Nineteenth Street Baptist Church — DC hours must not be imported.**
   Thursday 10am–1pm (2nd/4th Thursday) schedules found under this name
   belong to the Washington, DC church at 4606 16th St NW, not the
   Philadelphia church at 1253 S 19th St. The Philadelphia schedule is
   UNVERIFIED.
9. **Out-of-county listings on the Philadelphia page.** Four listings
   that appear on the Philadelphia page are outside Philadelphia
   County and are excluded: Bala Cynwyd (Montgomery), Sharon Hill
   (Delaware), Jenkintown (Montgomery), Glenside (Montgomery).

## What this report does NOT claim

- It is NOT a complete list of Philadelphia County food pantries.
  Philadelphia has hundreds of pantry/distribution sites (Philabundance
  alone works through 300+ partners); this pilot covers 26 in-scope
  listings plus 4 exclusions.
- It does NOT claim any pantry is currently open, closed, or defunct
  on the basis of a failed page load or a single aggregator label.
  UNVERIFIED means the public web evidence found was insufficient —
  nothing more.
- Hours taken from a church, office, warehouse, or community-centre
  page are NOT pantry hours, and are flagged as such wherever that
  confusion was possible.
- Secondary hours (Lemontree, foodpantries.org, place listings) are
  reported as secondary-only and must be confirmed with the pantry
  or PA 211 before publication.
- No pantry, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.

## Method and limits

- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-01. No pantry,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- A pantry's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party
  directories. Where a pantry's own site did not state an address or
  hours, that item is marked UNVERIFIED — a gap in public web
  evidence, not a finding.
- **No pantry is called defunct or closed in this audit.**
- PA 211 limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are
  JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-01 did not return a
  complete extractable Philadelphia County result list (one PA 211
  result was retrieved: Broad Street Love, meals only). The pilot is
  therefore anchored on that PA 211 item plus secondary lists, each
  item labelled with its claim source. A live-browser check of
  search.pa211.org for Philadelphia County is still outstanding
  before any send to PA 211.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the pantries themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
