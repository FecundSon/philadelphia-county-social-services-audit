## Method and limits

- Public web search and page-text fetch only; no contact with any organisation, no submissions.
- Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator.
- PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and a full Philadelphia-filtered PA 211 list could not be retrieved by text fetch, so this is a **partial pilot, not a complete directory** — anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus PCA and own-site lists. Centres not in this pilot are neither confirmed nor denied here.
- A Philadelphia postal address alone was not treated as proof of Philadelphia County; exact street addresses were used.
- Meal times are the least safe detail to quote: Summer 2026 voucher-distribution windows are not regular lunch times. Exact lunch hours per centre were not uniformly confirmed.
- Age thresholds are operator-specific (generally PCA 60+, NewCourtland 55+, city older adult centres 55+, KleinLife Active Adult Life 60+) and are not harmonised here.
- No rate-limit block was encountered in this run.
