# Philadelphia County — Senior services — Pilot list

- County: Philadelphia County, PA (city and county are coterminous)
- Category: Senior services
- Date checked: 2026-11-01
- Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
- Status: NOT SENT — staged only, for pre-send re-check
- Method note: PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch may be incomplete. This pilot is therefore anchored on PA 211 items that could be retrieved plus clearly-labelled secondary lists (notably Philadelphia Corporation for Aging — PCA — material and organisations' own sites). It is a partial pilot, not a complete directory of Philadelphia County senior services. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator.
- County rule: a Philadelphia postal address alone did not settle county. Exact addresses were used; listings in Montgomery, Delaware, Bucks, Chester, Camden and other neighbouring counties were excluded (see OUT OF SCOPE rows in the audit report).

## Notes and limits

- Meal-site days/times are the most change-sensitive detail in this category: several times above come from PCA seasonal voucher memos (e.g. Summer 2026 SFMNP distribution days) or older brochures, not from a current daily meal schedule. Do not present a voucher-distribution window as the centre's regular meal time.
- Senior-centre age thresholds differ by operator: PCA centres generally 60+, NewCourtland centres 55+, city older adult centres 55+, KleinLife Active Adult Life 60+. Do not harmonise them into one threshold.
- Commercial adult-day-care listings found in general search (e.g. in Center City) were not counted as senior community centres/services in this pilot.
- No organisation was called or contacted. No listing was called closed/defunct from a failed page load.
