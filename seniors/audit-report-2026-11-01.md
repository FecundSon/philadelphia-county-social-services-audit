The two listings are kept separate here; do not merge them or present either as the sole ombudsman contact until re-checked.
6. **One aggregator "Temporarily Closed" note was NOT adopted.** The Philadelphia Senior Center on the Avenue of the Arts (509 S. Broad St.) is listed as an active location, with Oct/Nov schedules, on NewCourtland's own current site. No own-site closure notice was found. Per the hard rules, a single aggregator status note does not establish closure.
7. **Three listings are UNVERIFIED and must be excluded from any send text** until re-checked: South Philadelphia Older Adult Center, PHA Emlen Arms and PHA Wilson Park. They rest on older guides only in this check; that is not a finding that they are closed.
8. **Meal times are the least safe detail to quote.** Several times in circulation are Summer 2026 farmers-market voucher distribution windows, not regular lunch times. Regular lunch is generally a weekday lunch, but exact lunch hours per centre were not uniformly confirmed — see the re-check list.

## What this report does NOT claim

- This is a **partial pilot, not a complete directory** of Philadelphia County senior services. PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and a full Philadelphia-filtered PA 211 list could not be retrieved by text fetch. The pilot anchors on retrievable PA 211 items plus PCA and own-site lists. Centres not in this pilot (for example PHA Cassie L. Holley, noted in older PHA material but not separately checked here) are neither confirmed nor denied by this report.
- **No listing is called closed or defunct in this report.** UNVERIFIED means only that a current own-site or directory confirmation was not retrieved in this check — including where a page failed to load or an aggregator status note conflicted with an own-site listing. The NewCourtland Avenue of the Arts aggregator "temporarily closed" note is explicitly not adopted.
- Hours, lunch times and seasonal voucher windows are **not interchangeable**. SFMNP voucher-distribution days/times (Summer 2026) are not regular meal times and are not presented as such above.
- Age thresholds are operator-specific (generally PCA 60+, NewCourtland 55+, city older adult centres 55+, KleinLife Active Adult Life 60+). This report does not harmonise them.
- No organisation was contacted, and no phone number, address or hour in this report was verified by a call or visit. "CONFIRMED" means confirmed against a published own-site or PCA source retrieved 2026-11-01, not by direct contact.
- Eligibility details that rest on a single secondary source (notably Caregiver Support sliding-scale/financial detail and the full eligibility test) are flagged CLARIFIED, not presented as programme-confirmed.
- County screening used exact street addresses. A Philadelphia postal address alone was not treated as proof of Philadelphia County.
