# Philadelphia County Mental Health / Crisis Services Audit — STAGED ONLY
**County:** Philadelphia County, PA (city and county coterminous).
**Category:** Mental health / crisis services.
**Date checked:** 2026-11-01.
**Status: NOT SENT.** Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide PA 211
or to any organisation. This is a staged audit for acceptance first.
**Method:** public web search + page-text fetch only. No organisation, PA 211,
or anyone else was contacted. An organisation's own website is treated as
authoritative over third-party directories; a directory beats an aggregator.
A failed or missing page is reported as UNVERIFIED, never as closure. No
organisation in this report is called defunct or closed. No crisis or
treatment advice is rewritten here — contact information only. This report
does not refer to the auditor as a local resident of Philadelphia County.

**Directory and pilot limit — read first.** The directory for any future send
is statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org). PA 211 search pages are
JavaScript-driven and text fetch may be incomplete. PA 211 records retrieved
in text form for this pilot were: Children's Crisis Treatment Center
school-based services, Center for Families and Relationships outpatient
therapy / Together Through Trauma, and Temple University Hospital–Episcopal
Campus Crisis Response Center. The rest of the pilot is anchored on the City
of Philadelphia DBHIDS Crisis Response Center page (fetched in full
2026-11-01), CBH provider lists, organisations' own sites, and clearly
labelled secondary lists. **This is a partial pilot, not a complete
Philadelphia County directory, and must not be presented as complete.**

**Crisis numbers first — a wrong digit here is not a minor typo.**
National 988 is kept separate from the Philadelphia/county crisis line
throughout. One does not replace the other.

## Headline findings (the ones worth sending on, once accepted)

1. **Philadelphia Crisis Line is 215-685-6440, 24/7 — CONFIRMED, and it is
   NOT 988.** DBHIDS's own CRC page lists the Mental Health Delegate
   Hotline 215-685-6440 (24/7) separately from "Call or text 988." Any
   listing that gives only 988 where the county line belongs, or only the
   county line where 988 belongs, is incomplete. Publish both, labelled.
2. **Friends Hospital CRC number CORRECTED to DBHIDS 215-831-2600 — with a
   widespread conflict to re-check.** DBHIDS (city source) gives
   4641 Roosevelt Blvd, Philadelphia, PA 19124, 215-831-2600. CBH, HealthyMindsPhilly,
   the School District, Main Line Health, Jefferson and the Free Library guide
   all give 215-831-4600 for the same CRC. Publish 2600 per DBHIDS, but
   re-check 4600 in a live browser before any PA 211 send — the split is too
   widespread to dismiss as one typo.
3. **Children's Crisis Treatment Center phone CORRECTED: 215-496-0707, not
   215-469-0707.** The own-site contact page (1080 N Delaware Ave, Suite 600,
   Philadelphia, PA 19125) and the PA 211 record both give 496-0707, with
   referral line 215-496-0584. A secondary guide's 469-0707 is a wrong-digit
   variant. Also: despite "Crisis" in its name, CCTC is not a 24/7 walk-in
   crisis centre — do not list it as one.
4. **Do not merge the CRC and outpatient entries that share a name or a campus.**
   Pennsylvania Hospital CRC = 801 Spruce St, 215-829-5433 (DBHIDS); Hall-Mercer
   outpatient = 245 S 8th St, 215-829-7271, scheduling 215-829-3461 (Penn Medicine).
   A secondary page's 245 S 8th St / 215-829-5249 CRC pair mixes the two and
   must not be copied. Likewise, adult HUP Cedar CRC (501 S 54th St,
   DBHIDS 215-748-9000) and CHOP children's centre (same street address,
   445-428-5800, ages 5–17, 24/7 walk-in) are distinct entries, and Friends
   Hospital CRC and Northeast Community Center (both 4641 Roosevelt Blvd
   campus) are different organisations with different phones.
5. **HUP Cedar adult CRC number CLARIFIED, not settled.** DBHIDS gives
   215-748-9000; a Penn Medicine place record gives 215-748-9525 (also the
   number in an older list under the former "Mercy Hospital" name for the
   same address). Publish DBHIDS 9000 for now; re-check 9525 on Penn
   Medicine's own CRC page before sending.
6. **Three Philadelphia numbers that look alike must not be interchanged:**
   215-685-6440 (Philadelphia Crisis Line / Delegate, 24/7),
   215-686-4420 (Philadelphia Suicide Crisis & Intervention Hotline, 24/7,
   DBHIDS footer), and 215-685-5400 (DBHIDS general line for uninsured /
   underinsured Philadelphians — not a crisis line). A secondary
   "Philly HopeLine 215-685-2449" was found in one list only and is
   UNVERIFIED; do not publish it as a crisis number.
7. **Urgent care is not crisis care — label it.** Merakey Adult Behavioral
   Health Urgent Care (3125 N Broad St, 267-219-8801, Mon–Sun 11am–6pm,
   adults 18+) and PATH Children's Urgent Care (1919 Cottman Ave,
   215-728-4651, Mon–Fri 1–4:30pm, ages 3–18) are both DBHIDS-listed
   voluntary assessment routes. Neither is a 24/7 CRC, and PATH's own page
   says involuntary/voluntary inpatient must go through the Children's CRC.
   NAMI Philadelphia's Warmline (1-844-PHL-HOPE) and CBH Member Services
   (888-545-2600, 24/7, Medicaid access) are likewise NOT crisis lines —
   NAMI's own page routes crises to 988.
8. **County-boundary trap, Philadelphia version.** Child Guidance Resource
   Centers, Havertown (2000 Old West Chester Pike, Havertown, PA 19083,
   484-454-8700) appears in a DBHIDS providers table and in Philadelphia-area
   searches, but Havertown is Delaware County — OUT OF SCOPE. Aviva Mental
   Health Services, Darby (MacDade Blvd, 610-522-4506) is likewise Delaware
   County and OUT OF SCOPE. Exact address, not search proximity, settles county.
9. **Hours are the weakest field.** For PMHC, Northeast Community Center,
   CATCH, Mental Health Partnerships and Horizon House, directories confidently
   give hours that the organisations' own pages (as fetched) do not state.
   Directory hours in this report are labelled directory-only and must not
   be published as organisation hours without an own-site re-check.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim to be a complete Philadelphia County mental-health
  directory. It is a 29-item partial pilot, anchored as described above.
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. UNVERIFIED means
  "not confirmed from the organisation's own site on 2026-11-01," including
  where a site was live but published no phone, address, or hours, and
  including the Friends Hospital place-record "Temporarily Closed" label,
  which is explicitly NOT adopted.
- It does not claim directory hours as organisation hours where the
  organisation's own site states none.
- It does not publish bed, placement, or waitlist availability, and it does
  not rewrite any crisis or treatment advice.
- It does not claim a Philadelphia postal address or a "Philadelphia area"
  search hit settles county — out-of-scope items were excluded by exact
  address.
- It does not refer to the auditor as a local resident of Philadelphia County.
- It does not present 988 as a replacement for the Philadelphia crisis line,
  or the Philadelphia crisis line as a replacement for 988.
