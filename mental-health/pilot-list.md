# Philadelphia County, PA — Mental Health / Crisis Services — Pilot List
Checked 2026-11-01. NOT SENT to PA 211 or any organisation. Staged only.

Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org).
Limit disclosed: PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch
may be incomplete. This pilot is therefore anchored on (a) PA 211 records
retrieved in text form — Children's Crisis Treatment Center school-based
services, Center for Families and Relationships outpatient therapy /
Together Through Trauma, Temple University Hospital–Episcopal Campus
Crisis Response Center — plus (b) the City of Philadelphia DBHIDS
Crisis Response Center page and CBH provider lists, and (c) clearly
labelled secondary lists. This is a partial pilot, NOT a complete
Philadelphia County mental-health directory. Do not present it as complete.

Philadelphia city and county are coterminous. Listings in Montgomery,
Delaware, Bucks, Chester, Camden and other neighbouring counties are
excluded, even when they appear in "Philadelphia area" searches. A
Philadelphia postal address alone does not settle county — exact
addresses were used. National 988 is kept separate from the Philadelphia /
county crisis line; one does not replace the other.

Contact information only. No crisis or treatment advice is rewritten,
summarised, or invented here.
