# Philadelphia County, PA — Mental Health / Crisis Services Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-01. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been
sent to PA 211 or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 29 mental health / crisis listings for Philadelphia
County, PA, checked against the organisations' own websites and other
public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory:
it does not claim to be a current list of open services, and it should
not be used as one without checking with the organisation or PA 211
first. Contact information only — no crisis or treatment advice is
rewritten here.

Counts: 29 items — 12 confirmed, 2 corrected, 11 clarified, 2
unverified, 2 out of scope.

**Pilot limit — please read:** PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven
and text fetch may be incomplete. PA 211 records retrieved in text form
were CCTC school-based services, CFAR outpatient therapy / Together
Through Trauma, and Temple/Episcopal CRC. The rest of the pilot is
anchored on the City of Philadelphia DBHIDS Crisis Response Center page
(fetched in full 2026-11-01), CBH lists, organisations' own sites, and
clearly labelled secondary lists. This is a partial pilot, NOT a complete
Philadelphia County directory.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-01. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No service is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure — including
  a place-record "Temporarily Closed" label for Friends Hospital, which is
  explicitly NOT adopted against DBHIDS's current 24-hour CRC listing.
- Hours are the weakest field: for PMHC, Northeast Community Center, CATCH,
  Mental Health Partnerships and Horizon House, directory hours are labelled
  directory-only where the own site states none.
- Pre-send re-checks (live browser) before any PA 211 submission: Friends
  CRC number/status, HUP Cedar number, Pennsylvania Hospital CRC number,
  Merakey phone, NAMI office address/hours, PMHC hours, COMHAR / CATCH /
  Mental Health Partnerships / Horizon House own-site contacts, Belmont
  Northeast and Philly HopeLine (confirm or drop), PCCRC age floor, and a
  live PA 211 Philadelphia list check (text fetch was incomplete).
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the organisations themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
- Directory for any future send: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org /
  search.pa211.org).
- This audit does not refer to the auditor as a local resident.
