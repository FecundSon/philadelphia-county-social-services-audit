# Philadelphia County, PA — Shelters pilot list
## Emergency, family, youth, veterans — checked 2026-11-01

**Status: NOT SENT. Staged only. Nothing in this file has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation.**

**Directory anchor:** Statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org). PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch returned only partial result lists, so this pilot is anchored on PA 211 items that could be retrieved in text form PLUS clearly-labelled secondary lists — principally the City of Philadelphia Office of Homeless Services (OHS) intake-centre page and the organisations' own websites. **This is a partial pilot, not a complete census of Philadelphia shelters.** Philadelphia city and county are coterminous; listings in Montgomery, Delaware, Bucks, Chester, Camden and other neighbouring counties are excluded even if they carry a Philadelphia postal address in an aggregator.

**How to read it:** "Directory claim" is what PA 211 / a secondary list says. "Own-site check" is what the organisation's own site (or, where no own site was retrievable, the City OHS page) says on 2026-11-01. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. Bed numbers are NOT published as static facts in this pilot — intake phone/process only, per the audit rules. Seasonal provision is flagged as seasonal.

**Confidential locations:** No confidential-location address is published in this pilot. Count of confidential-location addresses published: 0. Any listing whose address is confidential was excluded from listing rather than published.

## Notes for the next checker
- Philadelphia's system is intake-first: most City-funded shelters do NOT take walk-ins directly — placement goes through Apple Tree, Roosevelt Darby, the Veterans Multi-Service Center (Tue/Thu), or an after-hours centre. Any send to PA 211 must preserve that distinction and must not turn an intake centre into a "shelter you can walk into at any hour."
- Do not add bed counts from aggregators: Philly House, St. John's Coffee House, House of Passage and others all show conflicting counts across sources. Intake phone/process only.
- The auditor is not presented as a resident of Philadelphia County anywhere in this file.
