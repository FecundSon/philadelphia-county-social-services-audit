## Headline corrections

- **Philadelphia's system is intake-first.** Most City-funded shelters take placements only through an Office of Homeless Services (OHS) intake centre — Apple Tree Family Center (1430 Cherry St, 215-686-7150), Roosevelt Darby Center (804 N Broad St, 215-685-3700), or the Veterans Multi-Service Center on Tue/Thu — or an after-hours centre (Red Shield Family Residence for families, Mike Hinson Resource Center for single men, Gaudenzia's House of Passage for single women). Listings that read as direct walk-ins for referral-only shelters are misleading.
- **"Sunday Breakfast Rescue Mission" is now Philly House** — same site, 302 N 13th St, Philadelphia, PA 19107, 215-922-6400; men's 30-day emergency shelter, arrive before 7 p.m. for a bed.
- **Operator rename:** People's Emergency Center and Youth Service, Inc. merged into HopePHL on 2023-01-01, so older PEC/YSI names on shelter and youth listings are out of date.
- **Misclassified as walk-in emergency shelters:** Whosoever Gospel Mission is a 6–9-month residential programme for men 21+ (weekday screening intake), not night-by-night shelter; Station House is transitional housing by OHS referral and is no longer an after-hours intake site (that role is now Mike Hinson); Project HOME's Hub of Hope is a daytime drop-in centre, not an overnight shelter.
- **Phone/hours conflicts to resolve before any send:** House of Passage — current City number (267) 713-7778 vs older 215-634-9775 / 215-471-2017 / 215-849-7200; Mike Hinson hours — current City page 3 p.m.–11 p.m. vs older "daily after 5 p.m." lists.
- **Seasonal, not year-round:** Philadelphia Parks and Recreation Enhanced Code Blue overnight warming centres open only during an Enhanced Code Blue at rotating sites published on the City warming-centre map. The PA 211 contact address is not a shelter address.
- **No bed-availability figures are published** — sources conflict on capacity for several sites, so this audit gives intake phone/process only.
- **No confidential-location address is published in this audit.** Count published: 0.

## Method and limits

Public web only, checked 2026-11-01. Directory claims from statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org) were checked against organisations' own websites where retrievable, otherwise against the City OHS intake-centre page. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch returned only partial result lists, so this pilot is anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus City and own-site sources. **This is a partial pilot, not a complete census of Philadelphia County shelters.** Philadelphia city and county are coterminous; listings in neighbouring counties were excluded even when they appeared in Philadelphia-targeted lists. No organisation was contacted, and no organisation is called closed from a failed page load — unverified items are marked UNVERIFIED.
