# Philadelphia County, PA — Shelters audit report
## Emergency, family, youth, veterans

- **County:** Philadelphia County, PA (city and county coterminous)
- **Category:** Shelters — emergency, family, youth, veterans
- **Date checked:** 2026-11-01
- **Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
- **Status: NOT SENT.** Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation. This audit is staged only.
- **Method:** Public web only. For each listing, the directory claim (name, phone, intake method, who served, address if public) was checked against the organisation's OWN website where retrievable; otherwise against the City of Philadelphia Office of Homeless Services (OHS) intake-centre page. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. No organisation was contacted. No bed availability is published as a static fact anywhere in this report. Seasonal provision is flagged as seasonal, never presented as year-round. No organisation is called closed/defunct from a failed page load — failures are marked UNVERIFIED. The auditor is not described as a resident of Philadelphia County.

## Headline findings

1. **Philadelphia's shelter system is intake-first, and many directory listings hide that.** Most City-funded shelters do not take direct walk-ins: placement goes through Apple Tree Family Center (families/single women, daytime), Roosevelt Darby Center (daytime), the Veterans Multi-Service Center (Tue/Thu only for OHS intake), or an after-hours centre (Red Shield for families, Mike Hinson for single men, House of Passage for single women). Any correction sent to PA 211 must preserve that routing; a listing that reads as "walk in any time" for an OHS-referral shelter (Families Forward, HopePHL/Gloria's Place, Bethesda sites, Station House) is misleading.
2. **Three listings are misclassified as walk-in emergency shelters when they are not.** Whosoever Gospel Mission is a 6–9-month residential rehabilitation/job-readiness programme for men 21+ with a weekday screening intake — not a night-by-night emergency shelter. Station House is transitional SRO housing by OHS referral, and it is no longer an after-hours intake site at all (that role is now Mike Hinson Resource Center). Project HOME's Hub of Hope is a daytime drop-in centre, not an overnight shelter.
3. **Two renames make older listings stale.** "Sunday Breakfast Rescue Mission" is now Philly House (same address/phone: 302 N 13th St, 215-922-6400). "People's Emergency Center (PEC)" and "Youth Service, Inc. (YSI)" merged into HopePHL on 2023-01-01, so PEC/YSI operator names on shelter and youth listings are out of date.
4. **Phone numbers conflict for the single-women's after-hours centre.** Gaudenzia's House of Passage appears with 215-634-9775, 215-471-2017 and 215-849-7200 in older lists; the current City OHS page gives (267) 713-7778. The current City number should be used, and the older numbers should be flagged for PA 211 to retire — but only after the pre-send re-check below, because this is exactly the kind of detail that changes without notice.
5. **Hours have changed for the single-men's after-hours centre.** Older lists give Mike Hinson Resource Center as "daily after 5 p.m., 24 hours holidays/weekends"; the current City OHS page states 3 p.m.–11 p.m. and holidays/weekends. Flag for re-check before any send; do not send the older hours.
6. **A new after-hours family site appears on the current City page but not in older lists:** ACTS After Hour Intake Site, 1300 E Tulpehocken St, 267-297-7870. No PA 211 record text for it was retrievable, so it is CLARIFIED, not CONFIRMED, against PA 211.
7. **Age bands conflict for youth shelters and must not be sent as settled.** Covenant House PA is 18–21 on its own services page, 18–24 on its national page, and "under 21" in secondary lists. Youth Emergency Services is 12–17 on City lists but 9–17 in one HopePHL-era secondary entry.
8. **Seasonal provision is event-driven, not a fixed shelter.** Philadelphia Parks and Recreation Enhanced Code Blue overnight warming centres open only during an Enhanced Code Blue (3+ nights of Code Blue), at rotating sites published on the City warming-centre map. The PA 211 contact address (2101 Cecil B. Moore Ave) is an administrative contact, not a shelter address, and must not be sent as one.
9. **Partial pilot — stated plainly.** PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch returned only fragments, so this pilot (28 rows) is anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus the City OHS page and own sites. It is NOT a complete census of Philadelphia County shelters, and it must not be presented as one to PA 211.

## What this report does NOT claim

- It does NOT claim to be a complete list of Philadelphia County shelters. PA 211 text retrieval was partial (JavaScript-driven search pages); the pilot is 28 rows anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus City and own-site sources.
- It does NOT claim any bed-availability figure. Where sources conflicted on capacity (Philly House, St. John's Coffee House, House of Passage, Covenant House), no figure is published.
- It does NOT claim any organisation is closed or defunct. UNVERIFIED means "not verified from a retrievable own-site or current City source on 2026-11-01," not "closed."
- It does NOT claim walk-in access for OHS-referral shelters. Where a shelter takes placements only via OHS intake, the report says so.
- It does NOT claim Code Blue warming centres are year-round shelters, or that a Code Blue contact address is a shelter address.
- It does NOT publish any confidential-location address. Count published: 0.
- It does NOT present the auditor as a resident of Philadelphia County.
- It does NOT claim anything has been sent. Status is NOT SENT throughout.
